# Decision 227-3

- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decision:** In `cli/test/no-actions-mentions.test.mjs`, "never merge", "not merge", "nothing merges" and "don't merge" don't count as a negation of the CI they wait on, and in a sentence that has one, "without CI" asks for CI. "No merge" still counts, since "no merge waits on a workflow" rules the workflow out. A section is opt-in only when its lead starts "Only on", "Only for", "Only when", "Only with", "Only in" or "Only if" and names `ci = "actions"`.
- **Options considered:** split clauses at "if", "when", "whose" and "with"; drop a merge ban from the clause before testing for a negation; flag every clause that names merge and CI
- **Why:** Splitting at "with" breaks lines like "never ship with a workflow". Flagging every merge line would fail lines that say the merge never waits on a workflow. Dropping the ban catches all six cases from #228's round 3 review and keeps every line on main passing.
