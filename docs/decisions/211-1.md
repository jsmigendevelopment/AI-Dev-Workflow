# Decision 211-1

- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decision:** How #211 widens the "no approvals on Full" scan. It appends "ask the person to <any verb>", "ask what to do" and "stop and ask". "Ask first" counts when it is an order: ending a sentence as before, or mid-sentence at a clause's start, after a colon, comma, bracket or bold, or after "and", "then", "or", "so", "must", "should" or "always". A sentence-ending "ask first" whose subject is someone else ("We ask first.") or that names a topic ("What to ask first.") is no longer an ask. Lines it found got a below-Full condition and, where it matters, an On Full line; paying, the person's own logins and keys, and the always-stop items went into `ALLOWED`. python-fastapi's "**Ask first: does this need Python at all?**" became "**First question: ...**", since it is a question for the agent, not an ask.
- **Options considered:** A list of allowed verbs after "ask the person to" (misses the next verb); any verb (chosen); keeping main's "ask first" shape as it is and adding the mid-sentence one beside it (keeps the false alarms the review named); narrowing it with a subject check (chosen).
- **Why:** The owner's rule: on Full nothing waits on the person except paying, their own logins and the always-stop list. AC-211-2 checks that no sentence main flags passes the new scan, apart from the named non-asks.
