# Decision 454-1

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Decision:** How #454 brings rule 17 (decision 390-1) to the skills a person starts in the thread they talk to. (1) `build`, `pr-review`, `qa`, `diagnose` and `research` open with two short lines. The first: **Started by the person in the live thread?** Don't do the job there: start a background sub-agent briefed with the skill, the issue or the question and the person's words, say so in one line, end the turn, and pass the result on when it reports back. `build` passes the issue to the loop first if one runs, as `coordinate`'s "Where this runs" says for a job the person names. `diagnose` keeps an easy answer in the thread. `research` hands its step 2 research thread down, so the brief's commit happens there too. (2) `idea` and `join` say **This is the live thread**: questions, answers and reports stay; each long step goes down and the person is answered at once. `idea` names the scan and the wireframes (step 2), the repo (step 5), the plan and the issues (steps 9 and 10), and any install, scaffold or check; one prompt on Full hands the whole kickoff to one background sub-agent. `join` names the map (step 2), every install, build, test or check (steps 1 and 3) and the convention check (step 4). (3) The second line, the same in all seven: **Your brief came from another thread?** Never hand your own job down again: do it yourself, and start a sub-agent only where a step below says to. That keeps a builder, reviewer or validator from handing its work down again, and keeps the sub-agents a step already names (research's fact-check, idea's background scan, build's step 14 loop). (4) The two #455 review items: the pause or stop proof shows "the loop's own list when it reports back; don't wait for it" in `ask` and `coordinate`, and decision 390-1 part (2) now names `afk`'s handed-down commit and `back` step 9's no inline fix. (5) The tests are text tests, `cli/test/person-started-skills.test.mjs`; no eval scenario.
- **Options considered:** For where the line goes: a sub-bullet of rule 17 in `AGENTS.md` (one place, but the issue asks for a line in each skill, and `AGENTS.md` is outside the fence); a line inside a step (a builder reads it only once it is already working). For the sub-agent line: "hand nothing down again" (clashes with the sub-agents a step names, such as research's fact-check). For the tests: a new opt-in eval scenario per skill (seven real runs for one sentence each; the default run is at the 40-run cap); extending `live-thread-only-talks` with a second follow-up (it tests the live thread, not a sub-agent that must not hand down).
- **Why:** The opening is read before any step, by the live thread and the sub-agent alike, so both see which one they are. One shared second line is one word for one idea across seven skills. Text tests pin the wording where each reads it; #390's opt-in eval already covers the behaviour of handing down a job the person names.
- **Originator:** AI pick, not reviewed (Full automation: the owner, 2026-11-26). The rule is the owner's words in #390 (2026-11-27): "The thread I'm talking to should basically do nothing. It just reacts to me and answers me. All the work should happen in subagents and subthreads."
