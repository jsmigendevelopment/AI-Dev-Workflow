# Decision 213-1

- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decision:** How #213 item 4 tells "full" the word from Full the level. `namesFull` in `cli/test/approval-scan.mjs` still counts any capital "Full", and "on", "at" or "in full" in any case, unless that lowercase "full" is followed by an ordinary word other than "automation", "level" or "mode". So "at full speed" under a `**Below Full:**` label stays covered, while "at full," and "at full automation" are still flagged.
- **Options considered:** Leave "at full speed" flagged and explain why in the test (keeps a false alarm); a list of ordinary words to skip ("speed", "scale" and more; never complete); a negative lookahead that only lets the level words through (the pick)
- **Why:** One lookahead; every phrasing that names the level is flagged as on main, only lowercase "full" before a non-level word is let through.
