# Decision 392-1

- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decision:** How `lesson check --project` matches `y`/`ies` plurals, lower-case camelCase titles and counts out of a total (#392), on top of decisions 322-1 and 385-1.
  - **Plurals:** a term that ends in a consonant and `y` also matches its `ies` plural (`party` matches `parties`), and a term that ends in a consonant and `ies` also matches its `y` singular (`Memories` matches `memory`). An `ies` term keeps its plain `s` singular too (`movies` still matches `movie`), so it gives both forms. The same forms decide whether a term is a kit word that leaves the set.
  - **Titles:** a level-one doc title also gives a camelCase name that starts lower-case, such as `# iPhoneSync`, split into parts like any name (`phone`, `sync`, `i-phone-sync`, `i phone sync`). A one-letter part such as `i` is dropped, as before. In a sentence such a word is code (`useState`), so only a title gives it.
  - **Counts:** a number after `issue`, `PR` or `pull` and before `times out of` is a count, so `the same issue 3 times out of five` passes. `issue 41 times out` with no `of` is still an issue number. (Replaced by #410: `times out of` is a count only when a number follows `of`; decision 410-1.)
- **Options considered:**
  - Plurals: replace the plain `s` singular of an `ies` word with the `y` form (`movies` would no longer match `movie`, a new leak); add the `y` form beside it; or add every English plural rule. The extra form costs a rare refusal of a word such as `movy`, and fails closed.
  - Titles: lower-case camelCase names in titles only, in every doc line too (adds code names such as `useState`, so `use` and `state` would block common lessons), or not at all.
  - Counts: the review's lookahead (`times out` then `of` is a count), or no count rule after `times out`. A rarer phrase such as `issue 41 times out of the blue` passed as a count; it names no project detail beyond a number, but #410 refuses it.
- **Why:** Privacy first, without new false refusals. On a fresh app (`init` plus `add tanstack-start`, no install, made-up docs and author), 4 of 42 clean kit-style lessons were refused before this change and 2 of 42 after: the two `times out of` counts now pass, and the two left are `start` from TanStack Start in the docs. 6 of 3,939 common words are refused before and after.
